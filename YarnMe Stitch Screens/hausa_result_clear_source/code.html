Original source text (English):

Notice is hereby given to all residents and property owners along the proposed municipal highway expansion corridor. The regional government intends to commence surveying and preliminary land acquisition processes. Affected parties are required to submit their claims for compensation along with valid proof of ownership to the municipal planning office no later than November 30th. Failure to register within the stipulated timeframe may result in forfeiture of compensation rights.
