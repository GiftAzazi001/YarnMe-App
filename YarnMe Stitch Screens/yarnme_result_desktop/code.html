Understanding Treasury Bills in Nigeria

Treasury Bills (T-Bills) are short-term government debt instruments issued by the Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN). They are considered one of the safest investments because they are backed by the full faith and credit of the Federal Government.

Key Benefits for You:

  • check_circle Capital Preservation: Zero risk of losing your principal if held to maturity.
  • check_circle Tax Free: Interest earned on T-Bills in Nigeria is exempt from withholding tax.
  • check_circle Liquidity: They can be easily sold in the secondary market before maturity if you need cash urgently.

Currently, yields are relatively high, hovering around 14-16% for the 364-day tenor, making them an attractive option to hedge against inflation compared to standard savings accounts.
